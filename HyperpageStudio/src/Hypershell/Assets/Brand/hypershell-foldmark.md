## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: one production Windows desktop app icon for Hypershell, a modern dark coding, terminal, and creative workspace. Create a memorable bold H monogram made from two interlocking folded shell-like ribbons. The two upright strokes and connecting crossbar must read immediately as a capital H at 16-32 pixels; give their ends crisp diagonal cuts and a subtle forward-motion character. One simple sculptural emblem, not a detailed illustration. Place the large pale mint and warm ivory emblem on a single rounded-square deep graphite/forest-teal tile with a restrained mint edge. Flat front-facing graphic composition, strong contrast and generous but compact spacing, very subtle folded facet shading only. Tile fills 94 percent of square canvas, emblem fills about 72 percent. Genuinely transparent exterior outside tile's rounded corners. No words or extra lettering, no turtle mascot, no flames, no orbital swoosh, no repeated shell texture, no outer glow, no drop shadow, no device mockup, no icon sheet. Precise clean antialiased shapes, professional desktop brand mark, original design. Output one square icon only.

# Expanded mythology atlas · 4 October 2026

This cycle turns the five-canvas visual atlas into a connected reference collection and adds navigation needed to explore it. The original family overview and other opening compositions remain intact. A separate installed copy protects existing local projects and recovery drafts.

## Delivered collection

- 242 canvases; 3,245 content nodes plus 242 canvas roots; 1,486 relationships; 214 assets; 18 image placements; 92 containers.
- A genealogy catalog of 515 named individuals, nine collective records and five unnamed supporting parents. All twelve original Titans are included. Fifty alternative parentage records are distinguished from selected accounts.
- Zeus's register contains 98 offspring records: 94 individual figures and four collectives. These are attributed catalog entries, including alternatives, rather than a claim about one canonical biological census.
- All 24 books of the Iliad and all 24 books of the Odyssey have original accounts, event sequences, people, expandable interpretation, related-book connections and full public-domain translations. Twelve four-book movement maps, 17 journey stages and eight thematic comparisons connect the books.
- Twenty-one Greek/Roman comparisons, god dossiers with stories and descendant registers, and four Hesiodic works with 23 reading sections. Fragmentary and disputed attributions remain explicit.
- Original museum images with credits; searchable TXT/Markdown; raster and PDF previews; lossless SVG/audio attachments with download fallbacks; interactive Boolean logic.

The catalog is broad and sourced, not universally exhaustive. Ancient local traditions, unnamed groups, lost works and later accounts do not produce a single closed family tree. Full source notes identify passages and distinguish incompatible accounts.

## Product changes

Collapsed structural containers now become compact summary cards while preserving expanded geometry. Ordinary parent nodes retain their own content. Hidden descendants keep computing and sibling outputs remain visible. Project search spans canvases, hidden nodes and complete TXT/Markdown attachments. Search can reveal a result's ancestors; editor reveals are undoable.

The viewer adds an outline, original-node context, backlinks, Back/Forward and a responsive Explore drawer. Canvas cameras are remembered during the session. Full text attachments open beyond their preview length. Viewer controls, navigation and collapse operate on a temporary copy; downloaded publications retain authored defaults. Styled headings wrap, and light-background attachment previews use authored text colors.

Meridian remains an early working release. The complete requirement audit is [FEATURE-COVERAGE.md](FEATURE-COVERAGE.md). Reusable modules, numeric and signal/audio systems, transitive dependency packaging, snippets, a spatial/public repository, accounts and collaboration remain future milestones. PDF/DOCX text extraction and complete mobile authoring are not provided by this cycle.
